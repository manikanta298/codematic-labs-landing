# Codematic Labs dark multi-page rebuild

## Build
- Rework the shared shell into a persistent responsive header and minimal footer used across Home, Services, Why Us, Case Studies, Tech Stack, and Contact.
- Replace all light surfaces with a strict near-black system, restrained off-white type, dark bordered cards, and sky-blue/deep-navy gradient accents.
- Simplify the navbar identity to the interlocking gradient mark plus “CODEMATIC LABS,” without the circular badge or tagline.
- Move services, differentiators/process, projects, technology groups, and contact content into dedicated routes with working navigation and route-specific metadata.
- Keep service and project content in shared typed data collections so cards and detail views remain easy to maintain.

## Pages and media
- Home: immersive dark blue-toned hero, animated headline, seamless client marquee, selected capabilities, process preview, featured projects, and CTA.
- Services: configurable service grid with richer detail and engagement CTA.
- Why Choose Us: numbered differentiators, animated outcome counters, and Discover → Design → Deliver process.
- Case Studies: project listing plus routed detail views with imagery, category, result, and clearly marked sample content.
- Tech Stack: icon-led technology groups for frontend, backend, databases, and delivery infrastructure.
- Contact: focused email CTA and project-start prompts.
- Reuse the existing generated case-study covers and create one cohesive abstract blue-toned hero visual.

## Motion and quality
- Add staggered heading reveals, shared viewport reveals, subtle image scale/parallax, animated counters, and a reduced-motion-safe infinite logo marquee.
- Preserve strong mobile layouts, keyboard-accessible navigation, stable media sizing, and responsive typography.
- Verify every route, case-study detail link, mobile menu, motion behavior, metadata, and current diagnostics.

## Assumptions
- Existing project names, testimonials, logos, and results remain clearly labeled sample content until real client material is supplied.
- “Contact/CTA” will be a dedicated Contact page, while primary calls-to-action throughout the site link there.
